Jobs

One job runs at a time for the whole app; the others wait in the queue. A scheduled job waits for its start time; the app must stay running (and the laptop awake) for it to fire, and one whose start and Stop by both passed while the app was closed shows as missed. Pause finishes the item in progress and ends the worker (the GPU is freed); Resume carries on with the items still missing. Cancel stops at once, also while pausing (the item in progress is dropped); finished items are kept. Records live in {{ cfg.data_dir }}/jobs/, with one log per job.
